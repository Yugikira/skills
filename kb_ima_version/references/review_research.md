# Extraction of Review paper

To be honest, more accurate description should be consolidate the review paper into wikis.

Usually, the review paper is targeting research on a specific concept. And it is the best chance to consolidate existing wiki pages about the concept.

Different from extraction task for other papers, extraction of review paper should be following:

## Step 1

Position the core concept the paper is about, check if the knowledge base has page for the concept by reading the _index.md or search ima knowledge base - `concept`.

If no wiki page for the concept, go to step 2, else go to step 3.

## Step 2

Read the `concept.md` template, to fill in with the information from review.

Use concise sentences to conclude main points, and just record oldest 2 and nearest 3 papers for source.

We don't want our wiki page grows too long as a review paper.

Instruct to read the review if one needs more information.

For vairables/methods/related concepts/theories，check for existing pages to establish correct links.

**ONLY** create concept page for the main concept.

## Step 3

Compare the content of existing page, and update with the information from the review paper.

Use concise sentences to conclude main points, and just record oldest 2 and nearest 3 papers for source.

We don't want our wiki page grows too long as a review paper.

Instruct to read the review if one needs more information.
